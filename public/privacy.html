Privacy Policy

Chowkra (Chowka Bara) · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Chowkra game ("the app", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is operated by {{COMPANY_NAME}}.

1. Information we collect

  • Game account: a random player identifier created on your device, a 7-digit public user ID, your display name and avatar choice.
  • Optional details you add: gender and email address (only if you type them in your profile).
  • Gameplay data: coin balance and coin history (spins, rewards, entries, wins), wins, losses, experience points, referral code and who referred you, and in-game emoji reactions.
  • Technical data: IP address and connection timing needed to run real-time multiplayer games and keep the service secure. We do not keep server logs of IP addresses longer than needed for security (normally 30 days).
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, where permitted), IP address and ad interaction data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your game account, match you with other players and run games.
  • To keep your coins, levels and history, and show leaderboards (your name, avatar and score are visible to other players).
  • To give rewards (daily spin, rewarded videos, referrals) and prevent abuse of them.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.
  • To answer support requests you send us.

3. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process account and gameplay data because it is necessary to provide the game you asked for (contract). Personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can give or refuse in the consent form shown by the app and change later from your device settings.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google AdMob (advertising and ad measurement);
  • hosting providers that run our game servers on our behalf;
  • authorities, if the law requires it.

5. Virtual coins

Coins are a virtual in-game item with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money or real-world prizes and cannot be withdrawn.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account while you use it. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This immediately removes your profile, coin balance and history from our servers. Logging out without deleting leaves the old account in place; it is removed after 24 months of inactivity.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our servers can be encrypted (HTTPS/WSS) and access to our database is restricted. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Most of this you can do inside the app; for anything else, email us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
